# Layering recipes

How to approximate each of the original's key sounds from free samples plus a little synthesis. Sample names refer to entries in `../assets/sound-sources.json`. The generators (`pip`, `bell`, riser, sub thump, synthetic reverb) are in `web-audio.md`, section 4. Buses are `sfx` unless noted.

| Target | Layers (bus: sfx unless noted) | Timing and processing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pack tear** | (a) card-stock tear slice (steeltowngaming 537763 or BigSoundBank Torn paper #2–#9), (b) short air whoosh (Kinoton 427823) at -8 dB, (c) foil crinkle tail (Pixabay Foil Crinkle) at -12 dB, (d) optional soft sparkle tail (Mixkit "Fairy magic sparkle") at -14 dB, 120 ms late | Make the tear _follow the finger_. Start (a) on swipe begin. Map swipe velocity to `playbackRate` 0.9–1.3 and to the gain of (b). On swipe end, stop (a) with a 30 ms fade and fire (c) and (d). High-pass (a) at 150 Hz, gentle shelf +2 dB at 5 kHz. |
| **Pack pop (rare tell)** | one bubble-wrap pop or `pluck_001`, pitched -3 st, -10 dB | Fires 40–80 ms after tear completes, only when the pack contains ≥ ★ rarity. Keep it quiet. It is a tell, not a fanfare. |
| **Card slide out** | `card-slide-x` + `scroll_00x` at -14 dB | Pitch var ±60 cents, round-robin 8. |
| **Card flip (common)** | `card-place-x` (body) + page-turn flick (Mateusz_Chenc 519102 slice) at -6 dB | Flick first, body 20–40 ms later. |
| **Rare reveal chime** | flip (above) + glockenspiel arpeggio built from VSCO 2 CE samples. ★ = 3 notes (root, 5th, octave) over 220 ms. ★★ = 4–5 notes + sparkle layer. ★★★ and Crown = 6–8 notes over 600 ms + shimmer + soft low "bloom" | Schedule notes with `start(t + i*0.07)`. Each note's gain decays exponentially (`setTargetAtTime(0, t+0.05, 0.25)`). Send 20–30 % to a short bright reverb (ConvolverNode with a 1.2 s synthetic IR, see web-audio.md, section 4). Keep arpeggio in the music's key or in C major if the bed is ducked. |
| **The pause** | filtered-noise riser (synth: white noise → BandpassNode sweeping 400 Hz → 4 kHz over 700 ms, Q 2) at -12 dB + sine sweep 200 → 800 Hz at -18 dB, then **150–250 ms of true silence** (music already ducked), then the chime | The silence is the effect. Duck music to 0.2 during the riser. |
| **God pack** | low impact (Kenney `impactSoft_heavy` or a BigSoundBank punch, pitched -7 st, low-passed 200 Hz) + fanfare chime (Kenney `jingles_STEEL` or Pixabay Victory Bell) + long shimmer (Pixabay Fairy Dust Magical Chime tail) | Impact at t, fanfare at t+60 ms, shimmer at t+120 ms with slow 2 s fade. Haptic pattern (Android). |
| **Coin flip** | Flick transient (coin payout clink) at t. Spin: BigSoundBank coin-spinning #4 trimmed to the last 1.0 s, with `playbackRate` ramped 1.15 → 0.85 to suggest deceleration. Landing clink at t+1.0 s. Tonal tag 60 ms later (heads: two sine notes up a major 3rd, tails: one lower note with slight detune). | Loop the spin region if the UI animation is longer. |
| **Energy attach** | `glass_00x` (Kenney) at -8 dB + sine "tink" (synth 1.6 kHz, 80 ms exp decay) + Breviceps 450614 transient at -16 dB | All within 15 ms. |
| **Attack impact** | shared body thud (BigSoundBank Punch #x, low-pass 400 Hz, -4 dB) + type layer (such as a fire whoosh, a zap or a clang) + a 60 Hz sine "sub thump" 90 ms for big hits | Scale body gain with damage (for example +0 dB ≤ 30, +2 dB ≤ 60, +4 dB > 60). Type layer 10–20 ms _before_ body for whooshy types (fire, grass), simultaneous for zaps. |
| **KO** | heavier body + descending sweep (`phaserDown` or a synth sweep 600 → 150 Hz, 500 ms) + short whoosh | Duck music to 0.5 for 1 s. |
| **Evolve** | riser (synth or Pixabay "Magical Sparkle Rise") 700 ms → bloom chime (4-note major arpeggio, fast, 50 ms spacing) + `glass_00x` | Haptic pulse on bloom. |
| **Victory** | Kenney `jingles_PIZZI` or `jingles_STEEL`, or a Pixabay fanfare. The music bed is already mellow. | Duck bed to 0.3 under jingle. |
